🔥 Flash Your ESP32 Now

One-click installation. No Arduino IDE required. Pure degen technology.

HODL UP! Make sure your ESP32 is connected via USB and you're using Chrome, Edge, or Opera browser.

📋 How to Flash (EZ Mode)

  1. Connect ESP32 - Plug your board into USB port
  2. Click Flash Button - Hit that orange button above like you're buying the dip
  3. Select Port - Choose your ESP32 from the list
  4. Wait for Upload - Takes ~2 minutes (perfect for checking BTC price)
  5. Configure WiFi - Connect to "LNbits-Config" (password: 12345678)
  6. Open Portal - Go to http://192.168.4.1 and enter your LNbits details
  7. Stack Sats - Watch your balance in ultra-modern style 🚀

🔧 Supported Hardware

  • LILYGO T-Display S3 (recommended for degens)
  • ESP32 with TFT display (320x240)
  • Any ESP32-S3 with SPI TFT screen
